A FIELD GUIDE

How to explore

There is no set route, so go anywhere.
Every glowing spot holds a story.

Look around
Drag with your mouse or one finger to turn 360°.
Zoom
Scroll or pinch to zoom in and out.
Walk
Click the ground, or use WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, hold the arrow pad at the bottom left.
Open a section
Step onto a glowing circle to open a section. Clicking a section name or map marker walks you there.
Change the scenery
Switch between Forest, Desert and Ocean. Your place and content stay the same.

Lakes and the shoreline keep you on the island.
Press Esc to close any panel.

This island needs WebGL

You can still read everything from the sections below. Open the page in a browser with WebGL to walk around.
